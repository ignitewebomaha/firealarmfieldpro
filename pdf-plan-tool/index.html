Common questions

Can I mark up a fire alarm drawing on an iPhone or iPad?

Yes. The app imports PDF plans and supports device placement, addresses, text, arrows, and callouts. The PDF Plan Tool runs in the app, not in this website.

Can I export the marked-up PDF?

Yes. The app exports marked-up plans as PDFs for sharing. Review the exported drawing before using it for coordination or project records.

Can I try a PDF plan markup before subscribing?

The Free tier includes one PDF plan markup. Review the Free and Pro comparison for the other allowances and subscription options.
